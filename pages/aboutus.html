About Us

Pakistan Explorer Travel Agency is a travel company dedicated to promoting the beauty, culture, and tourism of Pakistan. Established in 2018, our agency has been helping travelers explore some of the country's most breathtaking destinations, including Hunza Valley, Skardu, Swat, Murree, and many other famous locations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide safe, affordable, and memorable travel experiences for tourists from across Pakistan and around the world. We believe that every journey should be enjoyable and filled with unforgettable memories. Through our services, we aim to showcase the natural beauty and rich heritage of Pakistan.

What We Offer

  • Family Tour Packages
  • Honeymoon Trips
  • Adventure Tours
  • Northern Areas Expeditions
  • Custom Travel Planning

Our Founder

Pakistan Explorer Travel Agency was founded by Ahmed Khan, a passionate traveler who wanted to make tourism more accessible and enjoyable for everyone. His vision was to create a company that helps people discover Pakistan's hidden gems while ensuring comfort, safety, and excellent customer service.

Our Offices

  • Islamabad
  • Lahore

Over the years, Pakistan Explorer Travel Agency has served thousands of travelers and continues to inspire people to explore the diverse landscapes, historical sites, and cultural attractions that make Pakistan a unique destination.
